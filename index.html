RaCutBall

Racquetball scoring for Apple Watch, for two or three players.

Keep score on your wrist without stopping play. RaCutBall handles singles and three-player cut-throat, which most scoring apps don’t.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • Two players: tap whoever won the rally.
  • Three players: tap Point or Side Out, and the serving order takes care of itself.
  • Faults and replays, so a second fault ends the serve.
  • Undo and redo, for when you tap the wrong thing.
  • Spoken scores and game results, so you don’t have to look.
  • Double-tap to score a point, on watches that support it.
  • Current runs during play, and the longest run after each game.
  • Side counts for your own side bets.
  • A summary when the match ends: time, games, wins, and more.
  • Stays on screen while you play, even alongside a workout.
