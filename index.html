RaCutBall

Cut-throat racquetball scoring for Apple Watch. Singles too.

Keep score on your wrist. I couldn’t find a watch app that scored cut-throat, so I made one.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Features

  • Three players: tap Point or Side Out, and the serving order takes care of itself.
  • Two players: tap whoever won the rally.
  • Standard or house rules, with best of 3 for two players.
  • Choose any serving order before a game.
  • Faults: a second fault ends the serve. Replays are supported too.
  • Undo and redo, for when you tap the wrong thing.
  • Spoken scores and game results, so you don’t have to look.
  • Input: score with a double-tap (on supported watches) or a turn of the Digital Crown.
  • Stats: see runs (points in a row on one serve) and unanswered points as you play, plus the best of each after every game.
  • Side counts: keep a tally of anything else you want to count, for each player.
  • A summary when the match ends: time, games, wins, and more.

Workouts

  • Record your session as a racquetball workout in Health, with heart rate and calories, and each game as its own segment.
  • An active workout keeps RaCutBall on screen while you play.
  • Start a workout with the Action button on Apple Watch Ultra, from Shortcuts, or with Siri.
  • Reminders if a workout is left running after you stop playing.
