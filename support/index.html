RaCutBall for Apple Watch

Scoring

The first time you open RaCutBall, a short walkthrough shows the basics. See it again any time in Settings › How to Use.

Starting a match

Choose 2 Players or 3 Players and tap Start. To change who serves first, tap the serving order before starting.

Two players

Tap the player who won the rally. If the server won, they score a point; otherwise the serve changes hands.

Three players

Tap Point when the server wins the rally, or Side Out when they lose it. The serve passes to the next player in order.

Faults and replays

Tap Fault for a fault serve; a second fault in a row ends the serve. Tap Replay when a rally is replayed, such as after a hinder.

Runs and unanswered points

The server’s tile shows their run: points in a row on this serve. A run ends when the serve changes hands. Unanswered points are the points they’ve scored since anyone else last scored. They carry over when opponents side out without scoring, and end when someone else scores. When unanswered points are more than the run, they follow it in brackets. For example, Run 3 (7) means 3 points on this serve and 7 unanswered. After each game and in the match summary, RaCutBall shows the longest run and the most unanswered points.

Mistakes

Use the undo and redo arrows at the top of the scoring screen.

Between games and ending a match

When a game is won, choose Next Game or End Match. In a best-of-3 match, winning the deciding game ends the match. To end a game or the match at any other time, tap … on the scoring screen. Ending a match shows a summary, and you can continue the match from there if you ended it by mistake.

If no game was completed, ending the match asks whether to save or discard it, and the summary also has Discard Match. Discarding deletes the match. The one before it becomes your last match.

Coming back to a match

An unfinished match reopens where you left off. If nothing has been scored for 30 minutes, RaCutBall asks whether to continue or end it.

Digital Crown

Turn on Digital Crown in Settings to score by turning the crown: up for the left player or Point, down for the right player or Side Out. A crown icon marks each. Each deliberate turn scores once, so a small bump doesn’t count.

Rules

Choose the rules in Settings › Rules. Changes apply to new matches; a match in progress keeps the rules it started with. The New Match screen shows which rules you’ll play.

  • Standard (USA Racquetball rulebook): games to 15, won by 1 point, and in two-player matches the first serve alternates each game.
  • House: games won by 2 points, the loser of a game serves first in the next, and in three-player matches the lower-scoring loser serves first.
  • Best of 3 (two players): the first to win two games. A third game is to 11; with standard serving, whoever has more points in the first two serves first.

You can also mix the options, and change the serving order before any game.

Workouts

RaCutBall can record your session as a racquetball workout in Health, with heart rate, active calories, and each game as its own segment. An active workout also keeps RaCutBall on screen while you play.

Starting and stopping

Swipe left from the score, or tap Workout on the New Match screen, to start, pause, or end a workout. When you start a match without a workout running, RaCutBall asks whether to start one, and when you end a match, it asks whether to end the workout.

Action button, Shortcuts, and Siri

On Apple Watch Ultra, choose RaCutBall in Settings › Action Button › Workout to start a racquetball workout with the Action button. Press the Action and side buttons together to pause or resume. The Start Racquetball Workout action is also available in Shortcuts, and you can say “Start a workout in RaCutBall.”

Forgotten workouts

If nothing has been scored for 10 minutes during a workout, RaCutBall asks whether you’re still playing. If you leave the app with a workout running, it reminds you after 10 minutes.

Turning workouts off

Turn off Workouts in Settings to hide the workout page. To change what RaCutBall can access, open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your picture, then Apps under Privacy, and choose RaCutBall.

Settings

Tap the Settings (…) button on the New Match screen, or Options (…) › Settings during a match, for player names, rules, spoken scores, double-tap, the Digital Crown, workouts, side counts, haptics, saved matches, and the walkthrough (How to Use).

Saved matches

Every match is saved on your watch when it ends. Settings › Saved Matches shows how many are saved and how much space they use, and can delete older matches or all of them.

Double-tap

On Apple Watch Series 9 or later, or Apple Watch Ultra 2 or later, with watchOS 11 or later, tapping your index finger and thumb together twice presses each screen’s main button: Point while scoring, Start on the New Match screen, and Next Game between games.
