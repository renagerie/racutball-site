Support

RaCutBall for Apple Watch

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email support@racutball.com.

Scoring

Starting a match

Choose 2 Players or 3 Players and tap Start. To change who serves first, tap the serving order before starting.

Two players

Tap the player who won the rally. If the server won, they score a point; otherwise the serve changes hands.

Three players

Tap Point when the server wins the rally, or Side Out when they lose it. The serve passes to the next player in order.

Faults and replays

Tap Fault for a fault serve; a second fault in a row ends the serve. Tap Replay when a rally is replayed, such as after a hinder.

Runs and unanswered points

The server’s tile shows their run: points in a row on this serve. When they’ve scored more than that since anyone else last scored, because opponents sided out without scoring, that total follows in brackets. For example, Run 3 (7) means 3 points on this serve and 7 unanswered. After each game and in the match summary, RaCutBall shows the longest run and the most unanswered points.

Mistakes

Use the undo and redo arrows at the top of the scoring screen.

Between games and ending a match

When a game is won, choose Next Game or End Match. To end a game or the match at any other time, tap … on the scoring screen. Ending a match shows a summary, and you can continue the match from there if you ended it by mistake.

Workouts

RaCutBall can record your session as a racquetball workout in Health, with heart rate, active calories, and each game as its own segment. A running workout also keeps RaCutBall on screen while you play.

Starting and stopping

Swipe left from the score, or tap Workout on the New Match screen, to start, pause, or end a workout. When you start a match without a workout running, RaCutBall asks whether to start one, and when you end a match, it asks whether to end the workout.

Action button, Shortcuts, and Siri

On Apple Watch Ultra, choose RaCutBall in Settings › Action Button › Workout to start a racquetball workout with the Action button. Press the Action and side buttons together to pause or resume. The Start Racquetball Workout action is also available in Shortcuts, and you can say “Start a workout in RaCutBall.”

Forgotten workouts

If nothing has been scored for 10 minutes during a workout, RaCutBall asks whether you’re still playing. If you leave the app with a workout running, it reminds you after 10 minutes.

Turning workouts off

Turn off Workouts in Settings to hide the workout page. To change what RaCutBall can access, open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your picture, then Apps under Privacy, and choose RaCutBall.

Settings

Tap … on the New Match screen, or … › Settings during a match, for player names, spoken scores, haptics, double-tap, workouts, and side counts.

Double-tap

On Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 2, or later with watchOS 11 or later, tapping your index finger and thumb together twice presses each screen’s main button: Point while scoring, Start on the New Match screen, and Next Game between games.
