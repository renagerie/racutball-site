Privacy Policy

RaCutBall for Apple Watch · Effective November 2026

RaCutBall doesn’t collect any information about you. Everything it uses stays on your devices and is never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app stores

Your matches, player names, and settings are stored only on your Apple Watch, and are removed if you delete the app. Workouts already saved to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Health data

If you choose to record workouts, RaCutBall asks for permission to:

  • Save workouts to the Health app, as racquetball workouts with each game as a segment, along with the active and resting calories burned during them.
  • Read your heart rate and calories during a workout, to show them on screen and to total each game’s calories.

This data is stored by Apple’s Health app on your devices, under your control. RaCutBall doesn’t send it anywhere, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. You can turn workouts off in RaCutBall’s Settings, and change or revoke its Health access at any time in the Health app on your iPhone (tap your picture, then Apps under Privacy).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, RaCutBall uses them only to remind you about a workout that’s still running after you leave the app.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of data.
  • No network connections of its own. Spoken scores are produced on the watch.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with Apple, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to app developers. These don’t identify you and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here before the app changes how it uses your data.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@racutball.com.
