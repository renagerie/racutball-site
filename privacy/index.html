Privacy Policy

RaCutBall for Apple Watch · Effective November 2026

RaCutBall doesn’t collect any information about you.

What the app stores

Your matches, player names, and settings are stored only on your Apple Watch. They’re never sent to us or anyone else, and you can remove them at any time by deleting the app.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of data.
  • No network connections of its own. Spoken scores are produced on the watch.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with Apple, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to app developers. These don’t identify you and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Changes

If the app ever needs to use your data, such as recording workouts in the Health app, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your permission.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@racutball.com.
